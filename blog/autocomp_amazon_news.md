# We were featured on Amazon News!

#### May 18, 2026

[Charles Hong](https://charleshong3.github.io/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} (UC Berkeley & Google)

### Link to the article: [https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/amazon-trainium-investment-university-ai-research](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/amazon-trainium-investment-university-ai-research){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

[Autocomp](https://github.com/ucb-bar/autocomp){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} was featured in an Amazon News article highlighting how university researchers are using AWS Trainium to push AI infrastructure forward through the [Build on Trainium](https://aws.amazon.com/ai/machine-learning/trainium/research/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} program.

> Professors Sophia Shao and Alvin Cheung are taking a different approach. Their system, Autocomp, uses large language models to automatically generate and optimize the low-level code that runs on Trainium—essentially using AI to extract the full potential from AI hardware. [...] Autocomp automates the entire process, iterating on code using real hardware performance feedback that doesn't require manual tuning.
>
> The next phase of the Autocomp project will extend the system from individual operations to full end-to-end pipelines for models like Llama and Qwen, running on Trainium's most powerful configurations.

Thanks to the AWS team for the feature and their continued support!
